Manyoha Plumbing Services provides a range of plumbing solutions for residential customers and small businesses. Our aim is to provide reliable and professional services that meet the plumbing needs of our customers.

Services We Offer

Leak Detection and Repairs

We assist customers with identifying and repairing common plumbing leaks to help prevent water wastage and further damage.

Blocked Drains

We provide assistance with blocked drains and drainage problems to help restore proper water flow.

Geyser Services

We provide geyser installation, maintenance and repair services for customers experiencing geyser-related plumbing problems.

Burst Pipe Repairs

We assist with damaged and burst water pipes to help reduce water loss and prevent further damage.

General Plumbing Maintenance

We provide general plumbing maintenance to help customers keep their plumbing systems working efficiently.

We provide assistance for urgent plumbing problems that require prompt attention.
